# Product brief

Problem: Make completed tasks and upcoming handoffs visible to authorized family.

Audience: families and non-clinical caregivers

## First release

One user role, one core record (handoffs) and one reversible workflow. Delay integrations and notifications until a backlog item justifies them.

## Guardrail

No diagnosis or treatment advice; minimize health data, require consent, revocation and audit.

Measure task completion, correction rate, offline recovery, trust, retention and deletion only after purpose and consent are documented.
